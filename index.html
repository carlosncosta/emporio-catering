We are Emporio Italia Catering, a premium catering service bringing the authentic soul of Italy to the Portuguese table. We specialize in curated Italian dining experiences, pairing handcrafted live pasta and artisanal regional dishes with exclusive Italian wines.

By sourcing directly from producers in Italy since 2020, we provide seamless, turnkey catering solutions for corporate events, private dinners, weddings and special events.

Armando Capocci

Executive Chef

Vice-President of the Italian Chefs Association in Portugal. Executive Chef of Lisbon's La Fiorentina, with a distinguished international career across France, Spain, and Italy. Trained alongside culinary icons Antonello Colonna and Niko Romito.

Alessandro Olivotto

Executive Chef

Originally from the Italian Dolomites, Alessandro bridges his background in Urban Planning with a profound passion for gastronomy. Since making Lisbon his home in 2013, he has led several of the city's most renowned Italian kitchens.
